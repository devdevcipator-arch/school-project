FAQ

Questions schools ask us

Everything about how Principals, Teachers and Students use the platform day to day.

Still have questions? Contact Us
How does the Principal monitor teacher attendance and daily activity?

Every teacher marks their own attendance with a check-in when they arrive and a check-out when they leave. Each action is timestamped and shows up instantly on the Principal's dashboard, where they can see who is present, late or absent, review daily activity, and view attendance trends over time.

How does the AI Test & Quiz Generator help teachers?

Teachers enter a topic, grade, number of questions and difficulty, and the AI generates a ready-to-use test or quiz in seconds. Teachers can review and edit the questions, then assign them to a class, saving hours of manual paper-setting each week.

Can the Principal send notices to specific roles (teachers vs. students)?

Yes. When creating an announcement, the Principal chooses the audience: teachers only, students only, or the whole school. Each notice appears in the right portal's notice feed, so everyone only sees what is meant for them.

How do teachers generate and send digital report cards to students?

Teachers enter or review student marks and progress, and the system builds a digital report card. With one click it is sent directly to the student's portal, where it can be viewed and downloaded. No printing or manual distribution needed.

How do students attempt online tests and access their results?

Assigned tests and quizzes appear on the student's dashboard. Students attempt them online within the given time, submit, and see their results once released, alongside their attendance records, notices and downloadable report cards.
